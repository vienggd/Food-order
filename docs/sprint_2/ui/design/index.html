Screens and states for F-05 menu management, F-06 cancel / reject, F-07 fulfilment and F-08 simulated payment. Same look as Sprint 1. Every page shows loading, empty, error and success states. The written rules and messages are in ui-spec.md.

Order status flow

One step at a time, no skipping, no going back (BR-13). Rejected and Cancelled are only possible from Placed.

Placed→Accepted→Preparing→Ready→Picked upPlaced→Rejectedby the café, with a reasonPlaced→Cancelledby the customer

Payment status flow (simulated)

The amount is always the order total from the server.

Unpaid→Paidcustomer presses PayPaid→Refundedautomatically when a paid order is cancelled or rejected (BR-16)

Badges

Order badges have a round dot, payment badges a square dot. Every badge has its word, so colour is never the only signal.

PlacedWaiting for the café
AcceptedCafé accepted it
PreparingBeing made
ReadyReady at the counter
Picked upCollected, finished
RejectedCafé said no, with a reason
CancelledCustomer cancelled
UnpaidNot paid yet
PaidPaid (simulated)
RefundedMoney returned (simulated)

Shared patterns

Confirm before anything that can't be undone; a short dark toast after success; a red message at the top for 409, then reload.

Confirm

Bottom sheet on phone, centred dialog on desktop. Safe choice on the left.

Toast

"Order #1040 rejected." Disappears after 4 s; also read out by screen readers.

409 conflict

Server message in bold "Not …" form, then the data reloads.

Simulated

A dark "Simulated" tag on every payment card and "(simulated)" on every amount.
